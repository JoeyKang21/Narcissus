# Narcissus

创建于2026年7月29日。  
昨夜与女友因死亡话题争执，忽忆起过往与前任们莫名争吵之情形，笔者不禁反思。为此，笔者尝试使用聊天记录、日记、随笔等信息，训练一个模仿自己风格说话之模型，体验一下与自己对话。今晨醒来，忽念此事，着手创建。思之西方神话中自恋致死之那尔喀索斯，故名之曰“Narcissus”。

Created on July 29, 2026.  
Last night, an argument with my girlfriend over the subject of death unexpectedly brought back memories of inexplicable quarrels with my exes. Me, the author, could not help but reflect.  
Thus, I attempted to use chat logs, diaries, notes, and other records to train a model that imitates my own manner of speech, hoping to experience what it is like to converse with oneself.  
Upon awakening this morning, the thought struck me, prompting me to begin its creation. Recalling the figure in Western myth who was consumed by self-love, I named the project accordingly: "Narcissus."
